AI in research

I founded and run the PSE Workshop on AI in Research — recurring sessions on protocols for safe and effective use of agentic coding tools in economics workflows.

01The workshop

Scope · protocols for safe, effective integration of large-model tooling into economics research workflows. Mode · open discussion, peer review of practice. Audience · PSE doctoral students, postdocs, and faculty.

We meet recurringly at the Paris School of Economics. If you're at PSE and want to join, or you're elsewhere and want to talk about replicating the format, get in touch.
